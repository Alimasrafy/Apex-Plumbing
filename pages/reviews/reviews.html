Planning your work

The thinking behind the fix.

Common situations to discuss when planning plumbing work.

A recurring kitchen blockage

For a recurring kitchen blockage, describe the pattern, affected fixtures, and previous clearing attempts. Discuss an inspection and a suitable clearing method rather than relying on repeated temporary fixes.

A better hot-water fit

When hot-water demand changes, compare household use with the equipment’s capacity and condition. Consider suitable repair or replacement options alongside installation needs and ongoing maintenance.
