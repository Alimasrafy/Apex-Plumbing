Emergency Plumbing in Austin & Nearby Texas Areas

When the unexpected happens, know your next step. Get practical help with urgent leaks and backups.

Understand the service

Calm guidance when every decision matters.

A burst pipe or overflowing fixture can quickly become more than an inconvenience. Start by protecting people, containing water only when safe, and arranging an assessment of the underlying cause.

Start with an assessment, discuss suitable options, and authorize a clearly defined scope. The details depend on your property, the condition of the system, and applicable local requirements.

What can go wrong

Common problems worth understanding.

Burst supply pipes

A sudden split can release pressurized water into walls or floors. Locate the main shutoff only if the route is dry and safe.

Overflowing fixtures

Stop using the fixture and keep children and pets away. Wastewater requires professional handling and sanitation.

Sudden loss of water

Check whether neighbors are affected and whether your utility has reported an outage before assuming an indoor fault.

Know the signs

When to arrange an assessment.

Uncontrolled water, sewage returning indoors, or a leak near electrical equipment needs urgent attention. Do not enter standing water near outlets or appliances.

Making an informed decision

Emergency response, without unrealistic promises

Urgent plumbing support is arranged after a direct conversation. Message Apex Plumbing with the issue and property location to check availability; no response or arrival time is guaranteed. For a gas smell, leave the area and contact the gas utility or 911 from a safe location.

Ask what the proposed work includes, what may only become visible after access is opened, and how changes to the scope will be approved. Keep a written copy of the agreed work and any applicable product warranty.

Should I shut off my water?

If you can reach the main water valve without entering an unsafe area, turn it off. Do not touch electrical equipment while wet or stand in water near electricity.
