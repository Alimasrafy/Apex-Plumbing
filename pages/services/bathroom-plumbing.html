Understand the service

Better bathrooms begin behind the walls.

A comfortable bathroom depends on what you see and what is behind the wall. Coordinate supply, drainage, fixture selection, and access before a repair or renovation begins.

Start with an assessment, discuss suitable options, and authorize a clearly defined scope. The details depend on your property, the condition of the system, and applicable local requirements.

What can go wrong

Common problems worth understanding.

Dripping taps and showers

Worn cartridges, seals, or damaged valve seats can cause persistent drips.

Slow tubs and basins

Hair and residue may restrict local drains; recurring problems need a broader check.

Uneven shower temperature

A worn mixing valve, pressure imbalance, or water heater issue can affect comfort.

Know the signs

When to arrange an assessment.

Drips, loose fixtures, moisture around tile edges, or repeated changes in water temperature should be investigated before cosmetic finishes are replaced.

Making an informed decision

Renovation plumbing, planned properly

Moving a shower or toilet can change drain gradients, vent requirements, and permits. Agree on fixture specifications and service access early, and complete inspections before closing walls. Waterproofing and structural work may require other qualified trades.

Ask what the proposed work includes, what may only become visible after access is opened, and how changes to the scope will be approved. Keep a written copy of the agreed work and any applicable product warranty.

Can I move a toilet to another wall?

It may be possible, but the drain route, venting, structure, and local permit rules must be assessed first.

Is plumbing the same as shower waterproofing?

No. Supply and drainage work is separate from the waterproofing system. Coordinate responsibilities between qualified trades to avoid gaps.

How do I request a quote?
