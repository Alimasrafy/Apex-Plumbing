What to keep in mind

Useful considerations for Round Rock.

Household hot-water demand

A growing household can outgrow its hot-water capacity. Compare simultaneous demand before selecting replacement equipment.

Hidden irrigation leaks

Separate indoor and outdoor water use when investigating unexplained consumption. Ask a qualified irrigation specialist about irrigation faults.

Pressure changes

Measure pressure under normal conditions rather than assuming low flow is a utility problem. A pressure regulator may need assessment.
