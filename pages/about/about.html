About Us

Good people. Thoughtful plumbing.

Get to know Apex Plumbing: clear conversations, practical service options, and thoughtful planning for your home or business.

Built on a simple idea

Service should leave you feeling reassured.

Apex Plumbing is built around a straightforward idea: customers should understand their plumbing problem and the work being proposed. We focus on useful explanations, practical options, and a service plan that respects the property and the people using it.

We handle enquiries for homes and commercial properties in Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, and Pflugerville. From the first description of an issue to an agreed scope, our aim is to make the next step clear—not to rush you into a decision.

How do I request a quote?

Use the enquiry form to prepare a WhatsApp message, or message +880 1718-658326 directly. Share the service needed, property location, and a suitable time to discuss the work. Your visit is arranged only after confirmation.

Will I know the cost before work begins?

We discuss the issue, proposed scope, and estimate before work is approved. Ask about assessment charges, materials, and any possible additional work so the plan is clear before you commit.

Do you offer residential and commercial services?

Yes. Our services cover residential and commercial plumbing needs in Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, and Pflugerville. Tell us about the property and proposed work so we can confirm the scope and scheduling.

When can I discuss a service request?

Our contact hours are 9:00 AM–6:00 PM. For urgent plumbing needs, message us to discuss availability. Confirm the appointment time and time zone when arranging a visit; a website enquiry is not a confirmed booking.
