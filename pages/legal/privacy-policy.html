About Apex Plumbing

This notice explains how Apex Plumbing handles information connected with this website and service enquiries. Our contact location is Dhaka, Bangladesh; our listed plumbing service areas are Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, and Pflugerville, Texas. Contact alimasrafi@zohomail.com with privacy questions. Effective October 16, 2026.

Information you choose to share

You may provide your name, phone number, email, service location, preferred date, and a description of the issue. Please share only what is needed to discuss the work. Do not include payment-card details, passwords, alarm codes, identification documents, or other sensitive information.

How enquiry information is used

Information you send is used to understand your request, communicate with you, discuss a proposed service or meeting, and handle related questions. Preparing the form happens in your browser. The website does not send the form in the background or create an appointment automatically.

Browser preferences and tracking

The site saves a manually selected light or dark theme in localStorage under apex-theme. It does not store form entries in localStorage or sessionStorage and does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts and images are hosted with the site. Your browser may cache website files.

WhatsApp, email, and external services

The form can create a link to WhatsApp with the details you entered. Opening that link shares its contents with WhatsApp so you can review and send the message; WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply. Email links use your chosen email provider. Use these services only if you are comfortable with their handling of your information. Their processing may take place in other countries.

Website storage and security

This static website has no enquiry database or server-side form endpoint. Messages you choose to send are handled through the relevant messaging or email service. Hosting providers may process technical access information needed to deliver and protect the website. No online channel can promise absolute security.

Keeping information

We keep enquiry correspondence only as needed for the discussion, agreed work, related administration, or applicable legal requirements. Exact retention depends on the nature of the correspondence and the relevant service provider. Contact us if you have a question about a particular message or would like us to review its retention.

Privacy requests

Email alimasrafi@zohomail.com to ask about information you have shared, request a correction, or discuss deletion where applicable. Include enough context to identify the enquiry without sending unnecessary sensitive information. Rights and any exceptions depend on applicable law; the relevant messaging provider may need to handle requests about its own systems.

Children’s information

Our services and enquiry channels are intended for adults and people authorized to arrange work for a property. Do not send children’s personal information through the form. If you believe such information has been shared, contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

Updates to this notice

We may update this notice when website functions, communication channels, or information-handling practices change. The effective date identifies the current version. Please review the notice before sharing information through a new feature.

Contact us

For privacy questions, email alimasrafi@zohomail.com. For service enquiries, WhatsApp +880 1718-658326 is also available. Contact location: Dhaka, Bangladesh. Please arrange any meeting in advance.
