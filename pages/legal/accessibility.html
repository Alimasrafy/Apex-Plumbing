Our commitment

Apex Plumbing aims to make its website useful to people browsing with different devices and assistive technologies. Accessibility is an ongoing process, and we welcome reports of barriers. No independent accessibility certification is claimed.

Design approach

The site uses semantic headings, readable text sizes, visible focus indicators, labelled controls, and responsive layouts. Its design targets WCAG 2.2 AA practices, but a target or an automated check is not a guarantee that every person or browser will experience no barrier.

Features available

A skip link leads to main content. Navigation exposes expanded states, FAQs use native disclosure controls, and forms provide labels and linked validation messages. The light/dark toggle is keyboard operable and honors an initial device preference. Reduced-motion preferences suppress decorative movement. Print uses a light palette.

Limitations to know about

The service-area selector is a schematic, not a geographic routing tool. Images are illustrative. The enquiry form needs JavaScript to prepare a message; direct WhatsApp and email links provide alternatives. External communication apps have their own accessibility features and limitations.

Request assistance

Email alimasrafi@zohomail.com or message +880 1718-658326 on WhatsApp if you encounter a barrier or need another way to discuss a service. Include the page, device/browser, and what prevented you from continuing. Avoid sensitive details. We can discuss an appropriate alternative communication arrangement.

Statement updates

This statement was updated October 16, 2026. We review website changes and reported barriers as part of ongoing improvement. Contact us if you need clarification about a specific function or the information on this page.
