About this policy

This policy explains browser storage used by the Apex Plumbing website. It covers the display preference saved on this site and links to external communication services. Effective October 16, 2026.

Cookies and browser storage

Cookies and localStorage are different browser technologies. Cookies can be sent with website requests; localStorage keeps a value for the current website origin. Browser caching may also retain files such as fonts and images to improve loading.

The theme preference

This site does not set first-party cookies or use sessionStorage. If you explicitly choose light or dark mode, apex-theme is saved in localStorage with the value light or dark. It stays on that browser and origin until you change or remove it. It contains no form details or customer identifier.

External communication services

Opening a WhatsApp or email link takes you to the selected app or provider. Those services may use their own cookies and storage under their own policies. No third-party map, video, review widget, advertising pixel, or analytics embed is included in this website.

Your display choice

Without a saved theme, the site follows your device preference. Changing the theme is optional. If storage is blocked, the toggle can still change the current page without saving the choice. No tracking-consent banner is used because this site has no optional analytics or advertising integration.

Managing stored information

Use your browser’s site-data settings to remove apex-theme, then reload to return to the device preference. You can block storage or disable JavaScript in browser settings. Core content and links remain available without JavaScript; use the direct WhatsApp or email links instead of the message-preparation form.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy if website storage or external integrations change. Any new feature should be assessed before it is enabled and this policy updated to describe its actual behavior.
